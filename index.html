Help

My Size Looks Wrong. Check the measurement on the Fit tab; the app warns you when a number looks like a typo or the wrong unit. Every size comes from the brand's own chart, so a brand that runs small or large says so on its page.

Plus, Trans Star Or Trans Festivale Isn't Unlocking. Tap Restore Purchases. If you have Plus, open TRANSformation once on the same phone, then go back to the companion app.

Cancelling Plus. Settings → your name → Subscriptions → TRANSformation Plus.

Market Problems. Report a listing or seller from its page. For an order problem, contact the seller first, then email us.

Deleting Your Data. In TRANSformation or Trans Festivale, go to Me → Delete my Market account. Device-only information is removed after you delete all three apps.
