Privacy Policy

TRANSformation, Trans Star And Trans Festivale · Effective 1 October 2026

TRANSformation helps trans people find clothes, sizes and gear that fit. Trans Star is its 18+ companion, and Trans Festivale is its festival companion. This policy covers all three apps. The short version: your body measurements never leave your device, we don't track you, we don't show ads, and we never sell anything about you.

What Stays On Your Phone

These are stored only on your device. Some are kept in a private space the three apps share so your preferences and purchases work across them. We never see them.

  • Your body measurements, their history and your notes
  • Your chosen name, unit preference, theme and app icon
  • Saved items, boards, goal measurements (Future Fit), fit notes (Fit Memory), Size Translator choices and alert settings
  • Whether you've confirmed you're 18 or older. If you confirm with Apple, Apple only tells the app an age range; if you enter a birthday instead, the birthday isn't kept, only the yes or no.
  • Your festival checklists, crew plan and campsite designs.

Deleting all three apps removes this local information. Deleting only one app may leave shared information available to the companion apps still installed.

Browsing Brands

Product photos and prices load straight from each brand's own website, and alerts check those websites directly from your phone. Those sites see an ordinary web request from your device, like any visit to their store; we don't send them anything about you. When you tap through to a brand, its own privacy policy applies.

The Market

The Market is optional and is the only part of the three apps that uses an account. If you use it, we keep:

  • Your sign-in. You sign in with Apple. We receive an account ID and the email Apple gives us, which can be Apple's private relay address.
  • Your public profile. The display name and photo you choose. Photos are resized and their location data is removed before upload.
  • If you sell: your seller name, pronouns, how you describe yourself (optional, your words), your bio, your listings and their photos. These are public to Market users.
  • Orders: which listing was bought, by which account, the price, shipping choice, our fee, and Square's order and payment references.
  • Reviews and reports you write.
  • Festival activity: festivals you say you're attending, whether you choose to be visible to fellow attendees, and meetup requests containing a public meeting spot, time and short note.

We never ask for or store legal names, ID documents, card numbers, bank details or shipping addresses. Square handles those.

Payments

Square processes Market payments. Sellers connect their own Square account, and Square confirms they are a real person; we only learn that they are verified. At checkout, Square collects the buyer's card and, for shipped items, their address, and shares the address with the seller so they can ship it. Square's own privacy policy covers that.

Apple processes TRANSformation Plus subscriptions and the Trans Star and Trans Festivale unlocks. We learn whether a purchase is active, never your payment details. TRANSformation Plus status is shared with Trans Star and Trans Festivale on the same phone so they can unlock.

Who Else Touches Your Data

  • Supabase hosts the Market's database and photo storage.
  • Square for Market payments and seller verification.
  • Apple for sign-in, age confirmation, purchases and notifications.

That's the complete list. There are no advertising networks, analytics services or data brokers, and we don't track you across other apps or websites.

Notifications

Plus alerts never name the item, so nothing personal shows on your lock screen.

Deleting Your Market Data

In TRANSformation or Trans Festivale, go to Me → Delete my Market account. That removes your sign-in, public profile and photos, seller connection, attendance and meetup data, and other Market content that isn't needed for a completed transaction. Records of completed orders and the minimum information needed for accounting, fraud prevention and disputes may be kept as long as legally necessary, with public profile details removed. You can also email us to ask for a copy or deletion of your data.

Children

TRANSformation and Trans Festivale aren't meant for children under 13, and the Market requires an Apple Account. Trans Star is only for adults 18 and older and checks age before it opens.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version goes here with a new effective date. Big changes will also be announced in the app.
